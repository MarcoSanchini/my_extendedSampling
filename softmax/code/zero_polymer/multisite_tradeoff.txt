=== (4) throughput: accepted mutations per move, vs the single-site baseline ===
Acceptance is estimated from the energy term alone,
    a_N ~ mean_trials min(1, exp(-dU_joint/T)),
which OMITS the Hastings proposal-ratio term (log a_BA - log a_AB). That
term is O(1) while exp(-dU/T) spans orders of magnitude, so this is the
right leading-order estimate, but it is an estimate and can err in either
direction -- it is not a substitute for running the real sampler.
Multi-site is worth building only where a_N*N exceeds a_1, shown as 'gain'.

Reading (4): a_N collapses roughly geometrically in N whenever dU per
mutated site is larger than T, because the N-site move must pay every
site's cost at once with no opportunity to reject them individually.
A single-site chain pays the same costs but gets N independent
accept/reject decisions, which is why it is hard to beat. Multi-site
can only win where dU/site is small relative to T -- look for that
regime in the per-drift table of section (2) and the T sweep above,
rather than assuming it exists.
